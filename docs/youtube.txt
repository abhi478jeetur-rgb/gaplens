TITLE
GapLens – Find What Your Sources Hide | AI Information Gap Detection | Shinchan Hackathon 2026

DESCRIPTION
GapLens checks your sources before you trust them. Ask one question, add 2 to 6 links, and it shows what's confirmed, what's missing, where sources disagree, which pages may be outdated, and what to verify next, with the exact quote behind every finding. English and Hindi. Free, no sign-up.

Try it: https://gaplens-nine.vercel.app
Code: https://github.com/abhi478jeetur-rgb/gaplens

Built for Shinchan Online Hackathon 2026, problem statement "Digital Dark Spot: AI-Assisted Information Gap Detection Platform".

How it works
• Reads each page on the server (Readability), with paste-text fallback for PDFs and blocked sites
• AI plans what a complete answer needs
• Every fact is matched to a verbatim quote, checked in code
• Coverage, gaps and conflicts are computed from verified evidence only
• Groq LLMs, Next.js 15, Vercel
